Features

Everything your server needs.

From keeping the peace to keeping your community engaged — PulseKeep has you covered with a comprehensive feature set that works out of the box.

Moderation Suite

Warn, purge, kick, ban, timeout, slowmode, lockdown, and announcements — all permission-gated and fully logged to your mod channel. Every action is auditable.

Support Tickets

Button-based ticket panels create private channels on demand. Add or remove members, close with full logging, and rename ticket channels on the fly.

Economy System

Daily streaks, work shifts, blackjack, slots, gambling, fishing, mining, robbing, and a full item shop with global leaderboard and voting rewards.

Auto-Moderation

Spam detection, mass mention protection, banned words, link blocking, and caps enforcement — fully configurable per guild with no setup required.

PostgreSQL Backed

Guild configs, command logs, economy balances, inventories, warnings, and analytics persist in a managed PostgreSQL database with encrypted connections.

Live Monitoring

Health checks, graceful shutdown, API-first status reporting, and a public status page with real-time metrics — all updated every 30 seconds.
