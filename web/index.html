Features

Everything your server needs.

From keeping the peace to keeping your community engaged — PulseKeep has you covered.

Moderation Suite

Warn, purge, kick, ban, timeout, slowmode, lockdown, and announcements — all permission-gated and logged.

Support Tickets

Button-based ticket panels create private channels on demand. Add/remove members, close with logging, and rename.

Economy System

Daily streaks, work shifts, blackjack, slots, gambling, fishing, mining, and a full item shop with leaderboard.

Auto-Moderation

Spam detection, mass mention protection, banned words, link blocking, and caps enforcement.

PostgreSQL Backed

Guild configs, command logs, economy balances, inventories, and warnings persist in a managed database.

Live Monitoring

Health checks, graceful shutdown, API-first status reporting, and a public status page with real-time metrics.
