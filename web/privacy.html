Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your data.

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you invite PulseKeep to your Discord server or use its commands, we may collect and store the following information:

  • Discord IDs: We store User IDs, Guild (Server) IDs, and Channel IDs to associate economy balances, settings, and logs.
  • Command Usage: We log when commands are executed for statistical and debugging purposes.
  • Message Content: We DO NOT log or store the content of your messages unless specifically required for a command to function (e.g., the /announce command temporarily processes your input to generate the announcement).

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for the operation and improvement of the PulseKeep bot:

  • To maintain your economy balances and cooldowns.
  • To provide the moderation and utility commands you request.
  • To display aggregate statistics on our website (e.g., total commands run).

3. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. Your data is stored securely in our PostgreSQL database.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your data. To do so, please join our support server and open a ticket. We will promptly remove your User ID and associated records from our database.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our Discord Support Server.
