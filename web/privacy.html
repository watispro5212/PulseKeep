Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. Information We Collect

PulseKeep stores only the data necessary for its functionality: Discord user IDs, guild IDs, channel IDs, economy balances, inventory items, and warning records. We do not collect personal identifying information beyond what Discord provides through its API.

2. How We Use Information

User IDs are used to associate economy balances, inventories, and warnings with specific Discord accounts. Guild IDs and channel IDs are used to store per-server configuration. Command usage is logged anonymously for statistics.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored in a PostgreSQL database. We implement reasonable security measures to protect this data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Retention

Data is retained for as long as the bot is in your server. You may request data deletion by removing the bot from your server or contacting the development team.

5. Third-Party Services

We use Discord's API and PostgreSQL. Your data is subject to their respective privacy policies.

6. Contact

For privacy concerns, join our support server or contact the development team directly.
