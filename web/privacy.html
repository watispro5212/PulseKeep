Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

Data PulseKeep may process

PulseKeep may process Discord user IDs, guild IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, command names, command timestamps, guild configuration, logging channel settings, ticket metadata, and economy balances needed to operate bot features.

Message content

PulseKeep does not sell user data. Message content is not stored for general analytics. Some command-provided text, such as announcement text or moderation reasons, may be processed to complete the requested command.

How data is used

Data is used to run moderation tools, audit logs, ticket workflows, economy commands, live status metrics, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, and service reliability checks.

Retention

Guild configuration and economy data may remain until a server removes the bot or requests deletion. Logs and command records may be pruned as the service evolves.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the support resources linked on this website.
